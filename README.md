## ✨ Key Features & Highlights

Wan2.7 is a comprehensive video foundation model that pushes the boundaries of visual storytelling:

* **Instructional Video Editing:** Recreate visuals, plots, and scenes with precise text prompts and multi-image guidance.
* **Creative Video Transfer:** One-click replication of dynamics, from complex character motions to cinematic camera work.
* **Seamless Time Extension:** Master the flow of time with frame-guided continuation and infinite video outpainting.
* **High-Fidelity Portraits:** Authentic facial details, from bone structure to subtle expressions, for unique character creation.
* **Advanced Text Rendering:** Support for long-form text, charts, formulas, and infographics across 12 languages.
* **Precise Color & Interactive Control:** Pixel-level alignment and full command over aesthetic color distribution.
